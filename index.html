Hi, I am Sid Raghavan

I am a fourth year ECE Ph.D. candidate in the area of Computer Vision at Purdue University. My advisors are Prof. Edward J. Delp and Prof. Fengqing Maggie Zhu.

I graduated with a master's degree from the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at University of Wisconsin–Madison, where I worked with Prof. Yin Li and collaborated with Prof. Andreas Velten.

My research interests broadly span across Computer Vision, Vision-Language Foundational Models and LLM's for science.
