Hi, I am Sid

A fifth-year ECE Ph.D. candidate in Computer Vision at Purdue University. My advisors are Prof. Edward J. Delp and Prof. Fengqing Maggie Zhu.

I earned my master's degree from the ECE Department at University of Wisconsin–Madison, where I worked with Prof. Yin Li and collaborated with Prof. Andreas Velten.

My research spans Continual Learning for Computer Vision, Multi-Modal Alignment, 3D Vision, and Reasoning Models.
